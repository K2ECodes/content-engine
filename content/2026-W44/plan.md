## Topics

1. **Your next customer is an agent.** Walmart opened its store to outside shopping agents while Amazon keeps blocking them (TechCrunch Oct 21, Retail Dive Oct 22), Meta Muse passed 10M users with $200+ baskets (TechCrunch Oct 21), Google Universal Cart went live (TechCrunch Oct 15). Why now: 3 of the biggest stores shipped agent shopping in 10 days, 4 weeks before Black Friday.
2. **Nothing sends without your yes.** Claude Duet preview with an approval step (TechCrunch Oct 22), Microsoft says treat agents like insider threats (TechCrunch Oct 19), OpenCode breach ran up AI bills and 1Password shipped agent logins (TechCrunch Oct 19 and 22). Why now: the approval gate we build into every system is now the industry default. Carries most workshop posts.
3. **Record parcel prices, so check the invoice before peak.** Q4 ground rates at an all-time high, 72 in 100 of fuel surcharge growth since 2021 from carrier pricing (Supply Chain Dive Oct 16), a brand hit with a $200,000 back invoice (Supply Chain Dive Oct 20). Why now: peak spending started in October (Deloitte via Modern Retail Oct 19) and volume hides billing errors.
4. **Your managers now manage agents too.** Nielsen survey for Monday.com: 58 in 100 managers expect to oversee more agents than people, 29 in 100 say their setup is right, 57 in 100 have more tools than last year (TechCrunch Oct 21). Uber puts 30,000 staff on ChatGPT Work by pairing 30 engineers with teams for 2 weeks (TechCrunch and OpenAI Oct 22). Why now: who owns each agent is the live LinkedIn debate.
5. **Skills are your SOPs, now portable.** Anthropic gave Agent Skills to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation: 70+ platforms, 1M skills, nearly 280M installs (Anthropic Oct 22). Skill-pack lead magnets are pulling the biggest comment counts in the niche (creator research, Oct 14). Why now: the freebie and the news line up exactly.
6. **What the 13 in 100 do differently.** BCG: only 13 in 100 logistics execs see measurable AI results (FreightWaves Oct 16), Motive, Samsara and Netradyne all shipped agents (FreightWaves Oct 13 to 22), OpenAI's Decisions API went wide (OpenAI and TechCrunch Oct 22), Five9: trust in support bots doubles when a person is reachable (Retail Dive Oct 22). Why now: logistics agent news has no creator coverage yet, and BFCM support volume is weeks away.
