Fact check (2026-11-27):
- Official: Google blog "Put Gemini to work for you" by Josh Woodward, Nov 17, 2026, https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/ ; Gemini release notes Nov 17, 2026 ("Tasks in Gemini, now available free of charge"), https://gemini.google/release-notes/ ; help pages https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507 and https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710.
- Free in the US, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India; elsewhere still Google AI Pro or Ultra; not in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. 18+, personal Google accounts (Workspace not yet).
- Previously "Gemini Spark": beta for US AI Ultra (May 2026), then AI Pro (July 2026). So "you had to pay for Pro or Ultra" is right.
- Start a task by flipping Chat to Task; runs in the background and notifies you. Mobile apps, Mac app and the web. Google's own examples include flight/hotel price monitoring, daily briefings, a surprise utility bill, comparing chargers and checking out with Google Pay.
- US extras (English, 18+): Gemini calls US businesses for quotes/stock/bills (you approve the call plan; not in IN, LA, MN, MT, NE), buys via Google Pay (nothing is bought until you approve, spending cap available), you can text Gemini via Google Messages / iMessage (opt-in, US number). Our research note's "texts on your behalf" was wrong: you text Gemini, it doesn't text people for you. Script says "you can even text it".
- Catch: it can use email, calendar, saved passwords, payment cards; asks before buying, sending, deleting, submitting forms or signing in; "Always allow" exists for routine actions. Free: scheduled tasks at most once a day, up to 10 schedules (paid 50). Gradual rollout.
- Press: TechRepublic, "Google Makes Gemini Tasks Free in 6 Countries, Adds US-Only Calls and Purchases", TechRepublic Staff, about Nov 19, 2026 (headline card; the research/daily source, whose Nov 17 date is Google's announcement date); also 9to5Google (Abner Li, Nov 17) and Android Central (Jay Bonggolto, Nov 18).
- The flight, price and checkout values on screen are an illustrative example, not real fares.
