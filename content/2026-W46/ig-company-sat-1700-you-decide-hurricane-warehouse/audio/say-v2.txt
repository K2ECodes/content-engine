You run a three P L warehouse in Tampa. A hundred and eighty people, two docks, and peak season starts next week. Then your phone buzzes.
A hurricane watch just went up for your county. Forecasters issue those about forty eight hours before tropical storm winds arrive. The port usually closes before a storm like this.
On the floor, three thousand orders are waiting to ship. Your biggest client is texting about Black Friday stock. Your shift lead wants to know if people can go home and board up their windows.
You get one move.
Option A. Run every shift and ship as many orders as you can before the storm hits.
Option B. Stop shipping now, move stock off the floor and up the racks, and send your people home to prepare.
Comment A or B. Part two shows what the storm does to each choice.
