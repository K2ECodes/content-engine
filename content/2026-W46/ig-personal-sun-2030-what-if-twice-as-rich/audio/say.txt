What would happen if you got twice as rich every single day? Day one. You have one dollar. You buy a pack of gum, and the guy at the corner shop doesn't even look up.
Day ten. Five hundred and twelve dollars. You take eight friends to dinner and pay for everyone. You've never been this popular.
Day twenty. Five hundred and twenty-four thousand. Your phone won't stop ringing. Cousins you've never met want to catch up.
Day thirty. Five hundred and thirty-seven million. You buy the building you've been renting in. Your old landlord asks if you're hiring.
Day forty-one. One point one trillion. You're richer than anyone alive. The bank manager walks you to the lift personally.
Day forty-eight. A hundred and forty trillion. That's more than the whole world earns in a year. Countries start calling you for a loan.
Day fifty. Five hundred and sixty-three trillion. You own more than all the wealth on Earth. There's nothing left to buy.
Day fifty-one. Your friend still slides you the bill.
