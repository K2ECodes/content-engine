## Script
What if you got $1 every time someone said AI? Out loud, anywhere on Earth, straight into your account. Day 1. A podcast plays at the gym. $14 before your second set. You buy yourself lunch and feel like a genius. Day 2. You go to 1 tech meetup. 2 hours, 4 slices of free pizza, $1,340. You never miss a meetup again. Week 1. Your friends work it out. Now they say it on purpose, then ask for 20%. 6 of them. Same group chat. Week 3. Your bank freezes your account for suspicious activity. $61,000, and you can't even buy a coffee. 9 calls from the fraud team. Month 2. Earnings season. Every CEO says it 80 times per call. You listen to 212 of them, just for fun. Month 6. $3.2M. The tax office sets up a whole team just for you. 14 people. They have a group chat too. Year 1. Companies panic and start saying machine learning, just to avoid paying you. It doesn't work. You're at $41M. Year 3. 3 governments try to ban the word. Tech conferences now sound like spy movies. $900M. Year 10. $14B. More than some countries. And you still can't explain what AI actually is. What should pay you next?
