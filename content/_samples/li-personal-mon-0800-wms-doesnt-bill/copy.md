## Caption
Most 3PL owners I talk to believe their WMS bills for everything.

It doesn't. It records everything. Those are different jobs.

Here's where the gap lives:

· Storage that rolls past the free period and never gets charged
· Accessorials done on the floor but never keyed in
· Kitting and project work quoted in an email thread
· Receiving discrepancies chased for a week, then dropped

None of it's fraud, and none of it's laziness. It's what happens when the record of the work and the invoice for the work live in 2 different places, and 1 person at month-end is the only bridge between them.

Run the math on a 3PL billing $250K a month. Miss 3 in every 100 dollars and that's $7,500 a month. $90K a year. Revenue you already earned, already paid labor for, and never collected.

The fix isn't another tool. It's a system that reads your WMS activity, matches it to each client's contract rates, and flags what's missing before the invoice goes out. A person approves every line. Nothing goes out without a human.

We build that layer on top of the WMS you already run.
