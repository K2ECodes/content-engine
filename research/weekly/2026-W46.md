## 1. The week in 5 bullets

1. Accenture's 20th annual holiday shopping survey (published Oct 15, still driving coverage through Nov 6) found about 3 in 10 shoppers would trust a personal AI agent more than themselves for holiday purchases, and the average amount shoppers would let an AI agent spend without asking first is $102. Nearly 7 in 10 want any AI agent to buy only from brands they already know. Sources: https://www.accenture.com/en/insights/consulting/consumer-holiday-research and https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/nearly-3-in-10-shoppers-trust-ai-more-than-themselves-for-holiday-shopping-100626.html
2. Shopify's Fall '26 Edition (released Oct 28) opened checkout on Shopify stores to outside AI agents, including Google's AI Mode and Gemini, Meta's Muse, Claude Duet and Grok's Instinct, using Shop Pay with buyer approval required. A store needs its terms of service, privacy policy and refund policy on file before in-chat checkout turns on. Sources: https://www.fudge.ai/blog/shopify-fall-2026-edition/ and https://blog.google/products/shopping/agentic-checkout-holiday-ai-shopping/
3. UPS, FedEx and Amazon Shipping's 2026 peak surcharges are all now in effect, running through mid-January 2027, with the per-package residential demand surcharge up roughly 25% year over year across the 3 carriers. Sources: https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/ups-preps-higher-holiday-surcharges-for-2026/828936/ and https://finance.yahoo.com/small-business/articles/amazon-shipping-readies-2026-holiday-100306067.html
4. Anthropic's public S-1 (filed Oct 26) showed its first quarterly operating profit, of $974M, on first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3B, and said its founders' widely reported 80% giving pledge is aspirational, not a binding commitment. Bloomberg reports the IPO roadshow could start as early as the week of Nov 9, the coming week, with a Nasdaq debut under ticker ANT targeted before Thanksgiving. Sources: https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-ipo-filing-nasdaq-ant-s-1 and https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-could-start-ipo-roadshow-week-of-nov-9-putting-debut-before-thanksgiving/
5. Walmart opened an Agent Gateway letting outside AI agents shop Walmart.com and Sam's Club with preset spending limits of $50, $100 or $200 and purchase approval required by default, while Amazon keeps blocking outside shopping agents such as Meta's Muse. Sensor Tower data cited by Axios shows agent-referred traffic to Amazon, Walmart and eBay is still under 1% of each site's traffic. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/while-amazon-blocks-ai-agents-walmart-rolls-out-the-welcome-mat/ and https://www.axios.com/2026/10/21/walmart-amazon-agents-muse

Also carried over and still live: UPS and FedEx's matching 5.9% average 2027 rate hikes (announced Oct 23, effective Dec 21 and Jan 4), Walmart's 14-day holiday sale window (Nov 12 to 15, Nov 23 to 29, Nov 30 to Dec 2, against 10 days in 2025), and the correction that the US-China tariff truce was already extended to Jan 10 2027, so the Nov 10 deadline cited in last week's scan no longer applies.
