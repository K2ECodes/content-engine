## 4. Trending formats and hooks seen this week

1. Specific, dated figures keep beating generic AI commentary: Salesforce's $75.8B Cyber Week figure, Adobe's day-by-day traffic jumps (141%, 95%, 88%) and the Overhaul theft count ($150M, 111 incidents) are each concrete enough to anchor a hook alone, matching research/creators.md's pattern of a specific money or scale outcome carrying the post.
2. LinkedIn's reach picture stays mixed and format-dependent: a large cross-post sample (Socialinsider, Jan to Mar 2026) still puts document and carousel posts well above text posts on engagement rate, while several single-creator write-ups this month claim text posts now out-reach carousels on the same information, a live disagreement worth tracking against our own post performance rather than switching format on anecdote. Sources: https://metricool.com/linkedin-algorithm-changes/, https://reeset.substack.com/p/linkedins-2026-algorithm-shift-why.
3. External links inside a LinkedIn post body reportedly cut reach by roughly 3 in 5, reinforcing our existing practice of keeping links out of post copy and using "link in bio/profile" or a comment keyword instead.
4. Posts that draw thoughtful, repeat comments reportedly earn about 3 times the reach of posts that only draw quick reactions, a direct case for Korede's planned daily commenting habit in research/linkedin-90-day-framework.md, not just for posting.
5. On Instagram, AI-narrated documentary-style "day in the life" reels and serialized numbered story series are both named as growing formats this month, a good fit for our existing what-if/you-decide series and realistic documentary-tone videos. Source: trend roundups dated Nov 2026, treated as directional only given vendor-blog sourcing.
