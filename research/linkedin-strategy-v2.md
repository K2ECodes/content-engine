# LinkedIn strategy v2: personal pages (Korede + Sanjana), Nov 2026

Source: Korede and Sanjana, 2026-11-25. The goal is more personal and less news reporting. News stays in the mix but is no longer the backbone. Both personal pages follow the 4-component model below. Reference posts are the screenshots Sanjana saved (operator-style AI deployment posts). Use them for format only, not for copy.

   - Mostly carousels with her photos: lifestyle, values, who she is.
3. **Technical depth, sprinkled in (about 15%, never the majority).**
   - The real problems leaders hit with AI right now and how we implement it, with technical terms that come up naturally (agents, MCP, evals, a data layer, SOPs vs reality, token costs, background agents).

4. **Being a young founder (about 20%).**
   - Something a mentor said, something she heard this week, a lesson from a hard week.
   - Setting a story in a plausible scene is fine ("last Tuesday a mentor told me..."). Never put words in a real, named person's mouth or name a real company in an invented scene.

## Format rules for both (from the reference screenshots + their notes)
- **Length:** short. Most posts are 80 to 180 words. Process posts must not turn into long essays.
- **Hook:**
  - First line is a real observation or scene: "Spoke to 20+ companies this month...", "Last Friday I sat with a COO who...", "Biggest issues I'm seeing with...".
  - Or a sharp contrarian line: "Most AI slop is just human slop."
- **Body:**
  - Body is a short list with → arrows or numbered lines.
  - Each item is 1 to 2 lines with a concrete detail (two CRMs, an acquisition that never got merged, an SOP that shows the ideal path, not the real one).
- **Close:**
  - Close with a 1-line takeaway ("Most of this comes down to process and change management, not the model.").
  - Then a genuine question or a soft ask ("Who owns the output of the AI at your company?", "Working on this? Drop a comment.").
  - Comment-keyword CTAs only on posts with a freebie; not every post.
- **Tone:**
  - Sounds like a person talking: contractions, the odd "IMO" or "prob" is fine.
  - No buzzword stacking. No em dashes.
  - Exclamation marks only when earned.
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5, not 12 to 15. Personal posts read better lighter.
- **Visuals:**
  - Wellness and founder posts carry photos of the person.
  - Process and technical posts work as text-only or as a clean PDF carousel. The "9 steps / save this" document style is fine, light theme, our type.
- **Cadence:**
  - Sanjana is posting aggressively: 1 a day, 7 a week (Mon to Sun). Mix: 3 people-side, 2 wellness, 1 founder, 1 technical. Rotate the founder and technical slots week to week.
  - Korede stays at 7 a week.
- **Context:** business-brief.md is the background for both. It sets what the company does and the founders' stories.
