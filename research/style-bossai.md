Korede: this is the new style and format for @koredekola.ai. He's recording a new clone so the face shots are high quality. Pay attention to how recent the stories are and to the lead magnets.

## What the format is
- **Freshness is the product.** He covers launches within about 24 to 48 hours ("X just dropped Y"). Most of his grid is Claude and ChatGPT launches. Topics come from research/daily "Hot today", same day.
- **Face only at the start and end.**
  - 0 to 3 s: a split screen. A graphic on top (the brand icon tile glowing on navy) and a real talking head below (him at his desk, natural room, cap). A caption sits on the seam.
  - Middle 75 s: no face at all. Full-screen motion graphics.
  - Last 5 s: the talking head returns for the CTA.
- **The graphics look:**
  - Background: deep indigo/navy radial gradient (about #0f0d1f to #1b1838).
  - The brand's own icon tile glows with a soft outline in the brand colour (Claude orange, ChatGPT black/white).
  - Flat Apple-style UI mocks: macOS windows with traffic lights, a dock, a Start menu grid, a prompt box that types the user's request letter by letter, app icons popping into a grid, progress bars, a cursor right-clicking a menu, dashed arrows, a red strike-through on "little widgets", tiny pixel space invaders under "most people are missing".
  - One real screenshot of the feature (the actual install dialog) and, on other reels, a real news-headline card (tomshardware) on white.
  - Everything is vector-clean and calm, no stickers, no rainbow.
  - About 1 visual change every 2 to 3 s, each acting out the exact words being spoken.
- **Captions:**
  - 1 line of 2 to 4 words, small white bold sans, centred just below the middle (about y 1010 of 1920). Plain, no box, no outline.
  - Only the current phrase shows, never stacked lines.
- **Voice:** calm, friendly, direct, first person ("I'm not gonna lie", "for example, I can just ask Claude to...").

## Script structure (85 s)
1. **Hook:** "<Brand> just dropped <feature> and this is big news." (0 to 4 s)
2. **What it is:** 1 sentence, plain words.
3. **Concrete personal example:** "For example, I can just ask Claude to build me a teleprompter that floats over my windows while I record". Show it step by step: prompt typed, it builds, right click, install.
4. **Twist:** "But here's the part most people are missing", then what it can really do (3 capabilities).
5. **Who gets it:** platforms, plans, "even the free one".
6. **1 honest caution:** "the one thing I'd watch out for is..."
7. **Zoom out:** "But think about what this means..."
8. **CTA lead magnet:** "If you wanna try this, comment APPS and I'll send you the link to this plus 10 apps you can build today."
   - The keyword is the feature's name.
   - The freebie is the link plus a bonus list (10 X you can do today).
   - The caption is 1 line: "<Brand> just dropped <feature> and this is big news. #ai #claude".

## How we copy it (our spin)
- **Template:** a new `ui-reel` template, separate from the stop-motion/glass story look.
  - Navy radial background, brand-icon tiles, a library of flat UI mocks (window, dock, start menu, prompt box with typing, icon grid, toggle list, progress, cursor, headline card, phone frame), word-anchored like the story template.
  - Captions: the single-phrase style above.
- **Face:** Korede's new clone (or real footage) only in the first ~3 s split and the last ~5 s CTA.
- **Voice:** Korede's own voice, the lively take (not the dry narrator).
- **Speed:**
  - Same-day news from research/daily.
  - Turnaround target: research in the morning, render and post the same day.
  - Freshest first always.
- **Lead magnets:** every reel gets a feature-named keyword and a "link + N things you can do with it" freebie, built into assets/freebies before it posts.
- **Caption:** 1 line plus 2 to 5 hashtags, in Korede's .ai style.
- **Volume:** 1 to 2 a day on @koredekola.ai, plus trials as usual.
- **Mirrors:** mirrored to TikTok Korede Kola.
