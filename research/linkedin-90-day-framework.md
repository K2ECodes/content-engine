## The framework
1. **Profile**
   - Clear headshot.
   - Banner with social proof and the company's value prop.
   - Plain headline: job title + company name.
   - Short first-person About section that says what you're building.
   - Featured section: case studies, press, product launches.
2. **Post 5 times a week**
   - 2 top-of-funnel posts: opinions on company building, team building, founder stories. These are for reach.
   - 2 middle-of-funnel posts: industry commentary, industry-specific opinions, frameworks and processes the ICP can steal, thought leadership.
   - 1 bottom-of-funnel post: case studies, testimonials, FAQ answers, objection handling.
3. **Connect with 100 ICP people a day**: ideally buyers, via Sales Navigator or a similar tool.
4. **Comment daily**: build a custom feed of the accounts the ICP follows and leave thoughtful comments there.
5. Repeat.
