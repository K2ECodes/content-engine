Purpose: copy the editing grammar of @stilesai for a Content Systems.ai GSAP/HTML reel template. We copy structure, layout, timing and motion rules only. Not his footage, mascot, words, music or branding.

## 2. Format

- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, 46 to 73 s (mean 60 s).
- One continuous selfie-camera take per video. Zero jump cuts inside the camera footage in all three (camera-region scene detection found nothing). He is a fluent one-take speaker; the edit never cuts the talking head.
- Footage: medium close-up, eye level, phone front camera on a desk or in hand, home interior, natural window light, holds a handheld wireless lav mic (furry windscreen) visibly in frame in two of three. No grade beyond phone default (warm, slightly overexposed).
- Two layout systems, evolving over the three posts:
  - Layout A "overlay cards" (DOOM): full-frame talking head; white cards sit over the top half of the frame (often covering his head); captions low.
  - Layout B "split canvas" (CAMPAIGN, AUDIT, the newer and current one): top 40 percent is a flat off-white canvas `#F0F0EC` (y 0 to 768), bottom 60 percent is the camera (y 768 to 1920, cropped from his 9:16 footage, head sits just below the seam). Captions sit ON the seam. For emphasis beats the camera disappears and the canvas fills the full frame.
- Ratio by screen time:
  - DOOM: 100 percent talking head on screen, about 75 percent of the time with a card over it.
  - AUDIT: 100 percent split layout, 0 full-screen graphics.
  - CAMPAIGN: about 62 percent split, 38 percent full-screen canvas (5 full-screen stretches: 17.7 to 23.3, 27.1 to 35.5, 39.1 to 45.6, 48.1 to 52.2, plus short ones). 13 hard layout cuts total.
- No B-roll, no stock footage, no AI video, no music video cutaways. All "B-roll" is flat vector motion graphics, plus one real screen capture framed in a card (DOOM).

## 4. Captions

### Style B (current, split layout, CAMPAIGN and AUDIT)
- Phrase chunks, not single words: 2 to 3 words per chunk (max about 18 characters), e.g. "I SPENT 2.2", "BILLION AI TOKENS", "THIS WEEK AND".
- Whole chunk appears at once, about 100 ms before the first word of the chunk is spoken (e.g. "BILLION AI TOKENS" on screen at 1.10 s, "billion" spoken at 1.22 s). Hard swap to the next chunk, no in/out animation, no active-word highlight. Chunk duration 0.6 to 1.1 s at his pace.
- Font: monospaced, bold, UPPERCASE, tracking about +0.10 em. Cap height about 36 px, so font-size about 48 px at 1080 wide.
- White `#FFFFFF` text on a near-black box `#0E1612`, box height about 80 px, horizontal padding about 28 px, square corners, no stroke, no shadow, width hugs the text.
- Position: horizontally centred, box vertically centred on the seam, y = 768 px (0.40 of height). Stays at exactly this spot when the canvas goes full screen.

### Style A (DOOM, older)
- Sentence case with punctuation, word-by-word ADDITIVE build inside a 1 to 4 word phrase ("You", "You guys", "You guys should", then clear, "let", "let your", "let your AI"), each word appears about 100 ms before it is spoken.
- Heavy grotesk sans (SF Pro / Inter, weight 800 to 900), white, about 90 to 100 px, soft dark drop shadow, no stroke, no box.
- Position: centred, baseline about y = 1405 px (0.73 of height). Exactly as his skill describes: "Word-by-word white subtitles, placed low".
- Persistent topic chip at top for the first 11 s: "Let your agents doomscroll", black heavy sans about 56 px on white with a 3 px black border, y 110 to 193.

## 5. Motion and graphics

- Camera: no zooms, no punch-ins, no shakes, no speed ramps, no whips. The camera is a static plate. All energy comes from the graphics layer.
- Transitions: hard cuts only. Split to full-screen canvas and back is an instant cut on a word onset. Card to card inside the canvas is an instant swap or a short fade (about 150 to 200 ms opacity).
- Graphic beat rate: a new graphic state every 2.4 s (CAMPAIGN, about 30 states in 73 s), 2.6 s (AUDIT, about 18 in 46 s), 3.5 s (DOOM, about 17 in 60 s). Every state change is tied to the noun or number being spoken.
- In-card builds (this is where the motion lives):
  - Number counters count up in 0.5 to 0.7 s and land on the spoken number (2.2B, 70 reels, 188 to 321 agents, 470M to 471M tokens).
  - Progress bars fill left to right in about 0.4 s (0 to 46 to 100 percent "Limit hit" in red; 0 to 50 percent "Only half my limit" in blue).
  - Lists and checklists add one row per spoken item, checkbox ticks on the word (AUDIT 33, 35, 37 s; DOOM "Transcripts, Frames, Captions" ticks as each is said).
  - Grids of small squares fill cell by cell (70 saved reels, 321 agents), then most cells dim to about 30 percent and a few "kept" cells stay solid.
  - Typing: a comment input types the CTA keyword letter by letter with a caret ("CAM", "CAMPAIGN"; "AUDIT"), about 8 to 10 characters per second.
  - Bar charts grow step by step (each step re-reads the chat).
  - Hand-drawn annotations in red script font fade in over 200 ms: "gross", "wrong", "save this one", "never opened", "not happening", plus a hand-drawn red circle around a tiny bar segment and a red X over a "$497 course" card.
- Graphic vocabulary: off-white canvas, thin black-bordered rectangular cards (1 to 3 px, square corners, a black header bar with tiny mono uppercase label), tiny mono "kicker" labels (about 22 px, grey `#777`), small mono footnotes ("SOURCE: MY CLAUDE LOGS, THIS WEEK") bottom-right of canvas, black step chips "1 . SAME CHATS FOR DAYS", red chips for alarming numbers ("ABOUT 1 BILLION"), green chips for "FREE" / "KEPT", blue filled chat bubbles for replies, red notification badge "99+".
- Mascot: a pixel-art robot (blue body `#1F52E1`, white eye band) that reacts per beat (holds phone, holds magnifier, multiplies into an army for "321 agents"). It is his brand asset; we need our own.
- Screen recordings: only DOOM uses one. A real Claude desktop screenshot sits inside a white card with a 3 px black border, full width minus 62 px margins, square corners, no device mockup, no rounded corners, mono footer "THE MEME | CLAUDE, SCROLLING REELS", one line gets a highlight box at 7 s.

## 6. Audio

- Loudness: integrated -14.1 to -14.4 LUFS (all three). Speech RMS about -17.5 dBFS.
- VO pacing: 3.9 (DOOM), 4.2 (CAMPAIGN), 4.0 (AUDIT) words per second. Mean inter-word gap 6 to 41 ms; pauses over 350 ms occur only 2 to 13 times per video, max 0.56 s. Fast, tight, but natural (one take, no cut-out breaths).
- Music: CAMPAIGN and AUDIT have no music bed (pause RMS -31 to -35 dBFS is room and breath; the tail after the last word drops to -44 and -47 dBFS). DOOM shows a faint steady tonal bed in the spectrogram and a -30 dBFS tail after speech, so a very quiet bed at roughly 18 to 22 dB under the VO is likely there. Either way music is at most a whisper.
- SFX: sparse. Spectrograms show only a few broadband transients per video, aligned with key graphic beats: AUDIT about 4.6 s (red limit bar slams to 100 percent), about 14.1 s (red circle drawn), about 41 s (keyword typed); CAMPAIGN about 8.6 s (red "wrong" strike-through) and about 65 s (keyword typed). Treat as: a soft click or tick on 2 to 4 key reveals, keyboard ticks on the CTA typing. No whooshes, no risers, no bass drops.

## 7. Colour and look

- His canvas: `#F0F0EC` off-white. Ink: `#111` / `#0E1612`. Primary data colour: royal blue `#1F52E3`. Alarm: red `#E53724`. Positive: green `#137C46`. Accent: yellow (bookmark, mascot antenna). Muted grey squares for "ignored".
- Headline numerals: very heavy grotesk, about 200 px cap height for the hero number.
- Overall: clean, editorial, "terminal meets Swiss print", lots of empty canvas, everything flat, no gradients, no glow, no glassmorphism, no 3D.

## 8. Structure

Typical beat map (from all three, timings normalised to a 45 to 75 s reel):

| Beat | Time | What he does | What the graphics do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | Number or claim plus emotion | Hero numeral counter or concept card, first annotation |
| Problem / tension | 3 to 10 s | "If you keep hitting your limit, this is probably why" / "most of you are doing it wrong" | Red bar, "wrong" scribble, "THIS IS PROBABLY WHY" chip |
| Proof / insight | 10 to 20 s | The surprising stat (94 percent, 0.3 percent) | Big bar, red circle, small red number |
| Steps | 17 to 50 s | Numbered "Step one, two, three" or "First, second" | Step chips top centre, full-screen canvas for each step, grids and counters |
| Payoff / my setup | 50 to 58 s | "So now my setup is..." | Checklist ticking item by item |
| CTA | last 6 to 10 s | "Comment AUDIT and I'll send you the prompt. It's free. If you get stuck, just message me." | Comment box typing the keyword, file chips (audit-prompt.txt, settings.json), green FREE chip, blue reply bubble |

CTA style: comment-keyword DM automation (CAMPAIGN, AUDIT), stressed as free and not selling ("I'm not selling anything, I promise"), plus a soft "just message me". DOOM used an engagement question card instead. The video ends on the CTA graphic with no outro card, so it loops.

## 9. Recreate it: template spec (1080x1920, 30 fps)

### Layout
- Default to Layout B. Canvas `#F5F0E8` (our cream) covering y 0 to 768. Media plate (talking head, screen recording or AI B-roll) y 768 to 1920, `object-fit: cover`, subject's head 40 to 120 px below the seam.
- Full-screen canvas state: hide the media plate with a hard cut (`gsap.set`, no tween) on a word onset; use on 3 to 5 emphasis beats per 60 s, each 3.5 to 8 s long, totalling 30 to 40 percent of runtime.
- Canvas content safe area: x 64 to 1016, y 120 to 700 in split; y 120 to 1640 in full-screen. Keep the caption seam clear (y 718 to 818).

### Captions (Style B)
- Font: JetBrains Mono 700 (alt Space Mono 700), 48 px, `text-transform: uppercase`, `letter-spacing: 0.10em`, line-height 1.
- Box: background `#17281C` (ink-green), text `#F5F0E8` (cream), height 80 px, padding 0 28 px, radius 0, centred, `top: 728px` (centre at y 768).
- Chunking: 2 to 3 words, max 18 characters, never split a number from its unit ("2.2 BILLION" can be "I SPENT 2.2" then "BILLION AI TOKENS" as he does, fine).
- Timing: show chunk at `firstWord.start - 0.10 s`, hide at next chunk's show time; hard swap, no tween.
- Emphasis variant (use 1 to 3 times per video): same chip with background `#C98A3C` (orange) for alarm words, `#9CCAA0` sage with ink text for "FREE" / "KEPT".
- Style A fallback (full-frame footage only): Rajdhani 700, 96 px, sentence case, cream `#F5F0E8` with `text-shadow: 0 4px 18px rgba(23,40,28,.55)`, centred, baseline y 1405, additive word reveal per word at `start - 0.10 s`, reset every 3 to 4 words or at punctuation.

### Graphic system mapped to our palette
| His | Ours |
|---|---|
| Canvas `#F0F0EC` | Cream `#F5F0E8` |
| Ink / card borders / headers `#111` | Ink-green `#17281C` |
| Data blue `#1F52E3` | Ink-green `#17281C` solid fills, sage `#9CCAA0` for secondary fills |
| Alarm red `#E53724` | Orange `#C98A3C` |
| Positive green `#137C46` | Sage `#9CCAA0` (ink text) |
| Yellow accent | Gold `#E8B84B` |
| Muted grey cells | `#17281C` at 15 to 25 percent opacity |
| Hand-drawn red script | Playfair Display Italic 600, 40 to 52 px, orange `#C98A3C`, rotate -4 to -6 deg |
| Hero numeral (heavy grotesk) | Orbitron 800 or Rajdhani 700, 200 to 240 px, ink-green |
| Mono kickers / footnotes | House rule bans eyebrows and kickers above headlines, so drop his kicker labels. Keep only source footnotes at the canvas bottom-right: Space Mono 400, 22 px, ink-green at 60 percent |
| Card body text | Rajdhani 600, 30 to 36 px |

Cards: 2 px solid `#17281C` border, radius 0, white or cream fill. His cards carry a black header bar with a tiny mono label; our CLAUDE.md bans eyebrows and series labels, so use a plain 12 px ink-green top rule instead, or put real content (a file name, a count) in the bar, never a category label. Never print pillar or execution names on any card.

### Motion (GSAP)
- Camera plate: static. No zoom, no shake. (If the source is AI B-roll or a screen recording rather than a talking head, allow a slow 1.00 to 1.04 scale drift over the whole shot, `ease: "none"`, to keep it alive; never punch-ins.)
- New graphic state every 2.5 s on average (range 1.5 to 4.5 s), triggered on the word onset of the noun or number it depicts.
- Card in: `opacity 0 to 1`, `y +12 to 0`, 0.18 s, `power2.out`. Card out: hard cut (`gsap.set`) when the next card replaces it.
- Counter: 0.6 s, `power3.out`, finish on the spoken number's word start; round to the displayed precision each tick.
- Bar fill: `scaleX 0 to value`, `transformOrigin: left`, 0.4 s, `power2.inOut`.
- List row / checkbox tick: one per spoken item, 0.15 s fade plus tick stroke draw 0.2 s.
- Grid fill: stagger 0.008 to 0.015 s per cell, `from: "start"`; dim pass to 0.2 opacity in 0.3 s.
- Annotation (script word, circle, X): DrawSVG style stroke draw 0.35 s `power1.inOut`, or opacity 0 to 1 in 0.2 s for script text.
- CTA typing: 9 chars per second with blinking caret (0.5 s period), then file chips pop in 0.25 s apart, then FREE chip, then reply bubble.
- Mascot or icon reaction swaps every beat (our own pixel or line mascot, not his robot).

### Audio
- VO at -14 LUFS integrated, peaks -1 dBTP. Target 3.8 to 4.2 words per second; trim pauses over 0.4 s.
- Music: off by default. Optional bed at -22 dB relative to VO (about -36 LUFS), lo-fi or minimal pulse, sidechain duck a further 4 dB under speech, cut dead at the last word.
- SFX cue list (all at -18 to -22 dB relative to VO, short, dry):
  1. Soft UI tick on the hook counter landing (0.6 to 1.2 s).
  2. Low thud or "error" blip when an alarm bar hits 100 percent / the orange chip appears.
  3. Marker scratch (0.3 s) on each hand-drawn annotation, max 2 per video.
  4. Single checkbox click per ticked item in the payoff list.
  5. Keyboard ticks under the CTA keyword typing.
  6. Nothing else: no whooshes, no risers, no transitions sounds on hard layout cuts.

### Script and beat template
Hook (number plus emotion, 0 to 3 s), problem (3 to 10 s), surprising proof stat (10 to 20 s), 2 or 3 numbered steps each with a full-screen canvas beat (17 to 50 s), "my setup now" checklist (50 to 58 s), comment-keyword CTA with "it's free" and "just message me" (last 6 to 10 s), end on the CTA graphic, no outro.

## 10. What we cannot recreate without his footage, and substitutes

- His talking head and voice. Substitutes, in order of preference:
  1. Our own one-take selfie recording of the founder (the style needs only a phone and a lav; it is the most authentic match).
  2. Screen recording as the lower plate (Claude Code, the Content Systems.ai dashboard), cropped to fill 1080x1152 with a 2 px ink-green border and mono footer label, like his DOOM screenshot card.
  3. AI B-roll from kie.ai (image to video, 5 to 8 s clips, desk / hands / interface close-ups in our palette) as the lower plate, with a slow 1.00 to 1.04 drift; keep it under 40 percent of runtime so the piece still reads as motion graphics, not stock.
  4. Full-screen canvas only (100 percent motion graphics) with TTS or recorded VO; works because about 40 percent of CAMPAIGN is already canvas-only.
- His pixel robot mascot: design our own (e.g. a gold `#E8B84B` and ink-green pixel or line-art character) or use simple icons; do not reproduce his.
- His real data screenshots (Claude logs, DMs): rebuild as our own HTML cards with our real numbers; never fabricate stats.
- His comment-to-DM automation is a platform tool (ManyChat style), not part of the edit; the template only renders the CTA graphic.
