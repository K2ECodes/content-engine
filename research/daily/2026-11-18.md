## 1. OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet, agents can buy but a human signs off on every purchase

- Date: Nov 13, 2026.
- Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/ and https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-agentic-payments-in-the-us
- Summary: OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, letting its "dots" agents complete purchases without ever seeing the shopper's real card number. Before any charge goes through, the person reviews the items and total and approves with a biometric check. Launch access is limited to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers 18 and older with an eligible US card, through American Express, Mastercard and Visa, alongside a Commerce Partner Playbook for merchants.
- Why operators should care: the biggest AI company just shipped the exact model we sell, AI does the work, a person signs off before anything spends money, as the default for agentic commerce.

## 3. Walmart's Agent Gateway welcomes outside AI shopping agents, inside spending limits the shopper sets

- Date: Oct 21, 2026.
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/while-amazon-blocks-ai-agents-walmart-rolls-out-the-welcome-mat/ and https://ppc.land/walmart-lets-ai-agents-buy-within-customer-set-limits-such-as-50-or-100/
- Summary: Walmart opened an early beta of Agent Gateway, letting third-party AI shopping agents buy on its site. By default every agent purchase still needs the shopper's sign-off, unless the shopper turns on a spending limit such as $50, $100 or $200, and Walmart's terms let it ask for extra approval even inside that limit.
- Why operators should care: 2 of the biggest retailers (this item and item 1) both shipped the same answer to AI shopping within weeks of each other, approval by default, a limit if you want speed, which is the strongest validation yet for selling systems on that same principle.
