## 1. Anthropic files its S-1, plans to list on Nasdaq as "ANT"

- Date: Oct 26 2026.
- Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html and https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm
- Summary: Anthropic publicly filed its IPO prospectus. First half 2026 revenue was $16.3bn, up from about $1.16bn in the same period of 2025, with 80% of that revenue usage based rather than subscription. Customers paying $100,000 or more a year rose from over 950 to over 11,900 year on year, and about 1,900 customers now pay $1M or more. The company still posted an $80.7bn net loss for the half, though most of that is a non-cash accounting charge tied to convertible instruments, not cash spend.
- Why operators should care: the lab a lot of businesses now rent AI tools from just showed, in a public filing, how concentrated and usage based its revenue is. That is the plain case for owning a system built on a client's own stack instead of renting a seat on someone else's roadmap.

## 3. Goldman Sachs: small business AI use hits 86%, but most of it stays shallow

- Date: Oct 22 2026 (widened window by 5 days, not yet used in our files).

- Summary: A Goldman Sachs survey of 2,474 graduates of its 10,000 Small Businesses program, fielded with Babson College in August 2026, found 86% now use AI at work, up from 56% in 2024 and 77% in 2025. ## 4. NRF forecasts the fastest holiday sales growth since 2021, topping $1.013 trillion

- Date: Oct 15 2026 (widened window by 12 days, peak season framing still active this week).
- Source: https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/nrf-forecasts-2026-holiday-sales-growth-as-consumers-remain-resilient
- Summary: The National Retail Federation, working with Oxford Economics, forecasts November and December 2026 retail sales will rise 4.2% to $1.013 trillion, up from 3.1% growth in 2025. That would be the fastest holiday growth rate since 2021. The forecast excludes auto dealers, gas stations and restaurants, and is not adjusted for the 2.5% core goods inflation NRF expects this year.
- Why operators should care: a bigger, faster peak season is exactly when billing leaks, invoice overcharges and support load show up hardest, so this is a clean, sourced reason to push the peak season checklist now rather than after Black Friday.
