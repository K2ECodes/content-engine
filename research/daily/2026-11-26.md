## 1. AI shopping agents are mainstream for this Black Friday weekend, and the trust and liability questions are landing in real time

- Date: Nov 25, 2026.
- Source: https://www.npr.org/2026/11/25/nx-s1-6048524/what-to-know-before-using-ai-agents-for-black-friday-shopping and https://political.org/2026/11/25/ai-shopping-agents-go-mainstream-for-black-friday-what-shoppers-should-know-before-handing-over-their-data/
- Summary: NPR and other outlets report AI shopping agents have gone mainstream for this Black Friday weekend, comparing prices and placing orders for shoppers across major platforms. Coverage flags real open questions: whether a purchase an agent makes counts as "authorized," how payment and account access should be limited, and bank warnings that shoppers may be on the hook for an agent's mistakes. A separate UK survey this season found roughly 6 in 7 people planning to use AI for holiday shopping would trust it to place and pay for an order automatically, even as fraud risk and picking the wrong item are their top 2 worries.
- Why operators should care: our own clients' stores are being browsed and bought from by agents this exact weekend, with no settled rule yet on consent or who eats the mistake, which is the entire argument for a human approval gate on anything an AI touches.
