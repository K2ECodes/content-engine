## 1. 2026 peak season carrier surcharges land meaningfully higher than last year

- Date: UPS published its 2026 demand surcharge schedule Aug 26 2026 (about 2 weeks earlier than its 2025 announcement); FedEx published its schedule Jul 22 2026; Amazon Shipping published its schedule Sep 2 2026. All 3 are in effect now and run through mid-January 2027.
- Source: https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/ups-preps-higher-holiday-surcharges-for-2026/828936/ and https://finance.yahoo.com/small-business/articles/amazon-shipping-readies-2026-holiday-100306067.html
- Summary: UPS and FedEx both set peak surcharge weeks for Nov 22 to Dec 26 2026, with rates in effect from late September through mid-January 2027. Amazon Shipping's 2026 fees run from Oct 25 2026 through Jan 16 2027 and, for the second year running, drop the volume-based surcharge it charged in 2024. Multiple trade sources report the per-package residential demand surcharge rose roughly 25% year over year across FedEx, UPS and Amazon Shipping.
- Why operators should care: this is the single biggest line-item swing in a 3PL or e-commerce brand's shipping cost this month, and it's already in effect, not a forecast.

## 2. Accenture's 20th annual holiday shopping survey finds a real trust gap behind the AI shopping hype

- Date: published Oct 15 2026.
- Source: https://www.accenture.com/en/insights/consulting/consumer-holiday-research and https://www.consumeraffairs.com/news/nearly-3-in-10-shoppers-trust-ai-more-than-themselves-for-holiday-shopping-100626.html
- Summary: Accenture surveyed over 4,000 US consumers and found about 3 in 10 shoppers say they would trust a personal AI agent more than themselves for holiday purchases, rising to 1 in 2 among active generative AI users. 4 in 10 shoppers expect to use generative AI this season, up from 3 in 10 last year, and shoppers expect it to influence 45% of their category spend. The average amount shoppers would let an AI agent spend without asking first is $102. Nearly 7 in 10 want any AI agent to buy only from brands and retailers they already know.
- Why operators should care: $102 is a real number founders can put next to their own approval-gate rule, and "only buy from brands I already know" is a direct argument for getting a store agent-ready now instead of after the fact.

That's the whole argument for building an approval gate into every system, not an afterthought." ## 3. Shopify's Fall '26 Edition opens checkout to outside AI shopping agents right before Black Friday

## 5. The returns rush is already starting, and retailers are leaning harder on AI to handle it

- Date: holiday customer-service survey data cited through late Oct and early Nov 2026.
- Source: https://www.ada.cx/media-center/press-release/ada-holiday-returns-survey-2026/
