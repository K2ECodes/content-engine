## 1. DOJ charges 3 men with using AI-cloned voices and faces to steal $129M from real companies

- Date: indictment unsealed Nov 6, 2026.
- Source: https://www.itv.com/news/2026-11-06/3-charged-in-scheme-to-use-ai-to-mimic-ceos-to-steal-129-million and https://www.scmp.com/news/world/united-states-canada/article/3373511/us-accuses-trio-using-ai-impersonate-ceos-and-lawyers-steal-us129-million
- Summary: The Southern District of New York unsealed charges against Jacob Soall, Sharon Hazan and Guy Sheetrit for wire fraud and aggravated identity theft. Prosecutors say the 3 used AI generated voices and video to pose first as CEOs, then as attorneys borrowing the identity of a real lawyer at a major international law firm, pushing accounting staff at target companies to wire money abroad before it was converted to crypto. The scheme ran from 2024 into early 2026 and totalled about $129M.
- Why operators should care: this is a real federal case, not a hypothetical, built entirely on the exact failure mode every approval gate exists to stop, 1 person in accounting trusting an AI voice or face enough to move money without a second human sign off.

## 4. Albertsons and Gap both widen enterprise AI deals that reach straight into customer checkout

- Date: Albertsons reported Oct 1, 2026; Gap reported Oct 7, 2026.
- Source: https://openai.com/index/albertsons-reimagining-retail/ and https://chainstoreage.com/albertsons-teams-openai-enterprise-ai-expansion and https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/07/gap-expands-ai-shopping-with-alta-daily-and-new-discovery-tools/
