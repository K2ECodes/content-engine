## 1. DHL Supply Chain says the bottleneck isn't the robot, it's the AI layer that tells every robot what to do next

- Date: Nov 9, 2026.
- Source: https://www.dhl.com/us-en/home/press/press-archive/2026/dhl-supply-chain-doubles-down-on-digitalization-strategy-pilots-adaptive-robotics-and-ai-orchestration.html and https://www.scmr.com/article/dhl-supply-chain-bets-on-data-foundations-robotics-and-agentic-ai-to-drive-growth
- Summary: DHL Supply Chain announced the next phase of its digitalization strategy: piloting adaptive robots that can respond to unfamiliar warehouse conditions, and moving "AI orchestration," a control layer connecting data, AI agents, robots and people, into live sites. DHL already runs more than 8,000 robots globally and plans to expand its modular robotics platform past 100 sites over 3 years, with a stated goal of doubling the business by 2030.
- Why operators should care: the world's biggest 3PL just said the win isn't buying a robot, it's the layer that tells the robot, the software and the people what to do next, exactly the gap most mid-size warehouses and 3PLs still have.

## 2. DoorDash and Amazon both say outside AI shopping agents still produce almost no orders

- Date: Oct 29, 2026 earnings calls.
- Source: https://fourweekmba.com/ai-amazon-doordash-ceos-say-few-shoppers-buy-through-ai-agents/ and https://stellagent.ai/insights/doordash-third-party-ai-agents-hundreds-orders
- Summary: On the same earnings day, DoorDash CEO Tony Xu said third-party AI shopping agents have produced fewer than 1,000 total orders, against 993M orders that quarter. Amazon CEO Andy Jassy said outside AI agents aren't meaningfully moving the business yet and few customers shop that way, while Amazon's own in-house tools tell a different story: interactions with Alexa for Shopping are up more than 5.7x year over year.
- Why operators should care: the 2 biggest platforms just said, on the record, that outside AI shopping agents barely move volume yet, while their own in-house AI tools are already doing real work, a useful reality check against any pitch that treats "AI shopping agents are already here" as settled.
- Post angles: (li-personal, static, belief_shift, hot-take) "DoorDash: under 1,000 orders came through outside AI agents last quarter. Out of 993M. Here's the AI shift that's actually already paying off for them." (ig-company, static, tools_vs_systems, mistakes) stat card contrasting the "agents are buying for you now" hype against the real number, pivot to what's actually working today, CTA comment SYSTEMS.

## 3. Zalando launches its first AI shopping agent, and still won't let it check out until 2027

- Date: Nov 3, 2026.
- Source: https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/03/retailer-zalando-launches-ai-shopping-agent-as-margins-squeezed and https://stellagent.ai/insights/zalando-ai-shopping-agent-extended-checkout
- Summary: Zalando unveiled Zalando Extended, which lets shoppers buy from outside retailers' catalogs, starting with home goods partner Westwing, inside its own app with an AI agent handling the purchase. The agent can't complete checkout itself yet. Zalando says that piece arrives in 2027. The launch landed alongside a third quarter gross margin miss and a 5% share drop.
- Why operators should care: a retailer with the money and engineers to build its own AI shopping agent is still giving itself a full year before it trusts that agent to finish a purchase, a sane pace for any smaller brand sizing up its own AI rollout.
- Post angles: (li-company, static, calm_playbooks, checklist) a short post on why Zalando is giving its own AI agent a year before it touches checkout, and what that says about the right pace for a smaller brand. (ig-company, carousel, operational_clarity, proof) "Even Zalando isn't letting its AI agent touch checkout yet" carousel.
