## 1. OpenAI says it will stop training frontier models on the live internet

- Date: Oct 23 2026.
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/23/openai-says-it-will-no-longer-train-its-ai-models-on-the-live-internet-citing-significant-risks/
- Summary: OpenAI said future frontier models will train on cached and simulated copies of the internet instead of crawling the live web, citing unpredictability and safety risk. The move follows Anthropic, which started limiting its newest models' live internet access about 2 weeks earlier.
- Why operators should care: the labs building these models no longer trust them unsupervised on the open internet, which is the exact argument for keeping a human approval gate on anything an AI employee sends or buys on a company's behalf.
- Post angles: (li-personal, static, belief_shift, mistakes) "OpenAI just said its own models cannot be trusted loose on the live internet. That is not a reason to fear AI. It is the reason every AI employee needs a yes button." (li-company, carousel, operational_clarity, checklist) "4 places an AI employee should never act without a human okay."
