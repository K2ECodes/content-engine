## 2. Enterprises are spending 2.5 times their planned AI budget this year, and agents are why

- Date: Menlo Ventures published its 2026 State of Enterprise AI report Nov 3 2026, based on a Morning Consult survey of nearly 400 US enterprise decision-makers fielded Sep 8 to 26 2026.
- Source: https://letsdatascience.com/news/menlo-ventures-report-puts-2026-enterprise-ai-spend-at-177-b-5671d63f and https://fourweekmba.com/ai-average-firms-ai-costs-hit-2-5x-budget-menlo-survey-finds/
- Summary: Menlo Ventures estimates enterprise AI deployment spend hit $176.8B through September 2026, up 5.4 times from $33B in 2025. The surveyed companies spent on average 4.7 times their 2025 level against a planned 1.9 times, a real-to-planned overshoot of about 2.5 times, and Menlo attributes most of the gap to AI agents running past their expected cost once they're live. Anthropic, OpenAI and Google together took $122.7B of that spend, 69% of the total, up from 50% a year earlier.

## 5. Document carousels are still the highest-engagement format on LinkedIn, and AI "broetry" is drawing a backlash

- Date: reporting on 2026 LinkedIn format data, most recently discussed through late Oct and early Nov 2026.
- Source: https://metricool.com/linkedin-trends and https://www.linkedfusion.io/blogs/linkedin-content-trends/
- Summary: Uploaded-PDF carousel posts on LinkedIn are running at roughly a 6.60% average engagement rate this year, the highest of any native format tracked, as more creators turn case studies and workflows into scannable, multi-slide breakdowns.
