## 2. Nvidia confirms it has stopped shipping licensed H200 chips to China, even with US approval in hand

- Date: disclosed on Nvidia's Nov 17, 2026 earnings call, widely reported Nov 17 to 18.
- Source: https://bdnews24.com/business/pyentjtvr9 and https://www.foxbusiness.com/fox-news-tech/nvidia-halts-china-ai-chip-shipments-despite-us-approval.amp
- Summary: Interim CEO Colette Kress told analysts Nvidia has halted shipments of its licensed Hopper-generation H200 chips to China and excluded them from guidance, after Washington had already approved sales to 10 Chinese firms. Nvidia sold $2.4B of these chips to China last quarter, and roughly 600,000 H200 units are reportedly sitting in Hong Kong awaiting Chinese import approval that hasn't come.
- Why operators should care: a shipment halt on the supply side, even with government approval granted, is a reminder that AI compute availability can shift fast for reasons outside any single company's control, worth watching for any knock-on effect on AI tool pricing next year.
- Post angles: (li-company, static, tools_vs_systems, mistakes) reassurance post: this changes nothing for the AI tools small operators already use, framed as why we build on what's already proven, not the newest chip.
