## 4. Google DeepMind has AI formally verify Signal's encryption code, catching the kind of bug a human review would miss

- Date: Nov 10, 2026.
- Source: https://deepmind.google/blog/formal-verification-signal-protocol and https://axios.com/2026/11/10/google-code-verification-signal
- Summary: Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code system generated roughly 6.5M lines of machine-checked proof over 4 weeks, with a team of 5 people, formally verifying the core of Signal's encryption protocol against the compiled binary rather than just the source code. A much smaller trusted base, about 26,000 lines, still needs human review.
- Why operators should care: the win here isn't "AI wrote the code," it's "AI's work got checked against a much smaller, human-reviewable piece," the exact shape of the approval gate Content Systems.ai builds into every client system.
