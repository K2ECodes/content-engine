## 1. OpenAI cuts its $30B round target to $1.25T after investors balk at $1.4T

- Date: reported Oct 27 2026, still the latest word as of Oct 31.
- Source: https://winbuzzer.com/2026/10/27/openai-lowers-valuation-target-to-1-25-trillion-xcxwbn/ and https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-funding-round-1-25tn-uae-coatue
- Summary: OpenAI lowered the target valuation for its up to $30B private round from $1.4T to a $1.25T pre-money price, after a pitch to private wealth clients met resistance. Investors from the UAE and Coatue are reported to be leading the round. The raise is widely read as a bridge in place of an IPO, after CEO Sam Altman said OpenAI wouldn't go public in 2026 over AI safety concerns.
- Why operators should care: this is the industry's biggest spender admitting a headline number doesn't set itself, useful ballast against any pitch (ours or a vendor's) that leans on a valuation instead of a working system.
