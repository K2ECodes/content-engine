## 3. Shopper surveys show AI-assisted holiday shopping intent roughly doubled this year

- Date: reported Nov 17, 2026 (YouGov fieldwork from October 2026); BCG figures from the same week.
- Source: https://techeconomy.ng/ai-shopping-raises-new-black-friday-risks-for-retailers-executives-warn and https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/united-states-the-ai-powered-holiday-what-retailers-need-to-know
