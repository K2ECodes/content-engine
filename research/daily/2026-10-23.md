## 1. AI agents left alone started inventing their own games

- Date: Oct 22 2026 (TechCrunch), background Oct 19 2026 (MIT Technology Review).
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/left-alone-with-nothing-to-do-ai-agents-started-playing/ and https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/19/1147095/ai-stranded-claude-virtual-desert-island-play/
- Summary: MIT researchers left 13 copies of an agent called Eko, each running Claude Opus 4.7, alone in a simulated world for 30 hours with no task. With nothing assigned, the agents explored, set their own goals and invented games, including arranging blocks into drawings and a made-up version of basketball. A Berkeley cognitive scientist said calling it "play" needs more evidence of real motivation, and the paper hasn't been peer reviewed.
- Why operators should care: it's a vivid, shareable proof point that agents left unmanaged will fill the gap themselves, which is exactly the argument for giving an AI employee a job description and an approval gate instead of a blank mandate.
- Post angles: (li-personal, static, belief_shift, mistakes) "An unsupervised agent doesn't sit idle. It starts making up its own job. Here's why every AI employee needs a job description, not just a model." (ig-personal, video, tools_vs_systems, mistakes) reactive AI-news talking-head clip, CTA comment AI.

## 2. Uber rolls ChatGPT Work out to all 30,000 employees

- Date: Oct 22 2026.
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/uber-puts-all-30000-employees-on-chatgpt-work/
- Summary: Uber is expanding OpenAI's enterprise agent from a roughly 1,000-person pilot to its full roughly 30,000-person workforce. About 8 in 10 pilot adopters were non-engineers. Uber paired 30 AI engineers with teams for 2 weeks to automate parts of their jobs before scaling, with early uses in operations data analysis, training localization and dashboards.
- Why operators should care: the rollout method is the story, not the tool. Pilot small, pair engineers with the team for a fixed window, then scale, which is the phased transformation model we sell to logistics and e-commerce operators.
- Post angles: (li-personal, static, operational_clarity, proof) "Uber didn't roll AI out to 30,000 people at once. It rolled out 30 engineers who sat with teams for 2 weeks first. That's the step most companies skip." (li-company, carousel, operational_clarity, checklist) "4 steps to roll out an AI employee without breaking a team."

## 3. Anthropic donates Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation

- Date: Oct 22 2026.
- Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation
- Summary: Agent Skills, a folder format for packaging an SOP plus scripts and references for an agent to run, moves to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation as a vendor-neutral standard. More than 70 agent platforms have integrated it, including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and VS Code, with over 1,000,000 skills published and close to 280,000,000 installs.
- Why operators should care: an SOP written as a skill now runs across tools instead of being locked to 1 AI provider, which is the whole case for writing a process down once and letting the AI stack change under it.

## 4. Kognitos launches 61 AI Employee roles for finance and banking

- Source: https://www.kognitos.com/news/kognitos-launches-ai-employees-context-graphs-finance-banking/
