## 4. Walmart opens a gateway for outside AI shopping agents, with spending limits built in, while Amazon keeps blocking them

- Date: announced Oct 21 2026, rolling out through November.
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/while-amazon-blocks-ai-agents-walmart-rolls-out-the-welcome-mat/ and https://www.adweek.com/commerce/walmart-embraces-ai-agents-that-shop-on-behalf-of-customers/
- Summary: Walmart launched Agent Profiles and an Agent Gateway that let outside AI agents shop Walmart.com and Sam's Club for a customer, with purchase approval required by default and optional preset limits such as $50, $100 or $200. Agents never receive stored payment card details, and each agent's activity is tracked on its own profile. Amazon, by contrast, has kept blocking outside shopping agents such as Meta's Muse from buying or scraping on its site.
- Why operators should care: Walmart just shipped a named, live example of "nothing spends past a limit without a person's yes," the exact rule every Content Systems.ai build runs on. It's a ready-made, credible proof point for that message, not a hypothetical.

## 5. Agent-driven shopping is still under 1% of site traffic, but the platforms are already repositioning their ad businesses around it

- Date: reporting from Oct 21 2026, still the live framing through this week.
- Source: https://www.axios.com/2026/10/21/walmart-amazon-agents-muse and https://www.emarketer.com/content/amazon-fights-back-ai-shopping-agents-threaten-ad-revenues
- Summary: Sensor Tower data cited by Axios shows ChatGPT referral traffic to Amazon, Walmart and eBay made up under 1% of each site's traffic in October, slowed by trust issues and clunky checkout flows. Even so, analysts tracking Amazon's ad business, which has been growing faster than its online store sales, say a shift toward AI agents doing the browsing and buying is a real threat to ad revenue built on people clicking.
- Why operators should care: the behavior change is tiny today, which is exactly the window to get a store agent-ready before it's table stakes instead of a late scramble.
