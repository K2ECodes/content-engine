## 2. Walmart stretches its 2026 holiday sale into 3 events and 14 days, up from 10 in 2025

- Date: announced Oct 26 2026, still driving holiday-prep coverage this week.
- Source: https://www.pymnts.com/?p=3198238 and https://bestreviews.com/articles/events/retailer-news/walmart-black-friday-deals-2026
- Summary: Walmart confirmed 3 separate 2026 holiday sale windows: Early Black Friday Deals Nov 12 to 15, Black Friday Deals Nov 23 to 29, and Cyber Monday Deals Nov 30 to Dec 2, a total of 14 promotional days against 10 in 2025. Walmart+ members get early access to select online drops in both Black Friday windows.
- Why operators should care: a 40% longer promo calendar from the largest US retailer resets what "peak" even means this year. A brand or 3PL planning staffing, inventory and ad spend around a single Black Friday weekend is already 2 weeks behind Walmart's own plan.
- Post angles: (li-personal, static, belief_shift, mistakes) "Walmart just turned 1 Black Friday weekend into 3 separate sales spanning 14 days. ## 3. A new survey finds AI-assisted holiday shopping has roughly doubled year over year

- Date: YouGov report published Oct 7 2026, still cited in trade coverage through this week.
- Source: https://yougov.com/en-us/reports/55672-us-mega-sales-report-2026 and https://www.retaildive.com/press-release/20261007-one-third-of-black-friday-and-cyber-monday-shoppers-expect-to-use-ai-but-f/
- Summary: YouGov finds 34% of likely US Black Friday and Cyber Monday shoppers say they're likely to use AI to help navigate this year's sales, against 16% who said the same about mega sales events last year. Of shoppers who used a standalone AI tool for last year's sales, 92% plan to use AI as much or more this year, including 46% who plan to use it more.
- Why operators should care: this is self-reported shopper intent, separate from the AI-traffic numbers we've already flagged, and it says the behavior is sticky, not a 1-season spike. A store that still reads as confusing to an AI assistant is turning away a growing, repeat-visit slice of holiday traffic.

## 5. USPS's 6% peak season surcharge is already running, but the regulator still hasn't ruled on it

- Date: filed with the Postal Regulatory Commission Aug 25 2026, status as of Sep 24 2026 reporting, surcharge window already in effect.
- Source: https://www.supplychain247.com/article/usps-2026-peak-season-package-rate-increases/ELD and https://www.freightwaves.com/?p=579288
- Summary: USPS filed a temporary 6% surcharge on Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express, Ground Advantage and Parcel Select with the Postal Regulatory Commission under Docket CP2026-10 on Aug 25 2026. The window runs from Oct 4 2026 to Jan 17 2027 and stacks on an 8% transportation surcharge the Commission already approved in April. As of the most recent reporting, the Commission's ruling on the 6% filing wasn't confirmed, even though USPS has been charging it since Oct 4.
