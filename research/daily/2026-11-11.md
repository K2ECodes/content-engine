## 2. CoreWeave's earnings beat eases the AI infrastructure selloff, but the debt keeps climbing

- Date: reported Nov 10, 2026, stock reaction through Nov 11, 2026.
- Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/coreweave-crwv-q3-earnings-report-2026.html and https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/11/coreweave-crwv-outlook-earnings-ai.html
- Summary: CoreWeave, 1 of the biggest AI cloud computing providers, reported Q3 revenue near $3.66B, almost 2.7 times what it booked a year earlier and ahead of the roughly $3.55B Wall Street expected, and raised its full-year revenue guidance to $13.0B to $13.3B. Shares jumped as much as 13% in premarket trading on the news, as investors read the beat as proof AI infrastructure demand is still real, a relief after the stock had lost close to 2 in 5 of its value over the past month in the broader AI selloff. Interest expense is still climbing toward $1.05B to $1.15B for Q4 as the company keeps borrowing to build data centers.
- Why operators should care: even 1 of AI's biggest infrastructure winners is burning cash and paying steep interest to keep pace with demand, a reminder that "the AI boom" and "a profitable AI business" aren't the same claim, whether the business is a cloud giant or a tool built on top of it.
- Post angles: (li-personal, static, belief_shift, mistakes) a hot take on reading an earnings beat as proof the hype is settled, versus reading the interest expense line underneath it. ## 4. Peak season carrier surcharges are already live, and the steepest window starts in 2 weeks

- Date: schedules published through Oct 2026, surcharges running since late Sept, steepest window Nov 23 to Dec 27, 2026.
- Source: https://supplychaindive.com/news/fedex-unveils-2026-peak-season-fees-higher-home-delivery-prices-loom/826049 and https://www.easypost.com/blog/peak-season-surcharges-2026/
- Summary: FedEx's demand surcharges have been running since Sept 28 and step up further from Nov 23 to Dec 27, with per-package additional handling fees now $8.80 to $11.85 and residential ground peaking near $0.80 a package, up roughly a fifth from last year. UPS runs a similar 3-step surcharge from Sept 27 to Jan 16, with an extra residential fee for shippers moving more than 20,000 packages a week, and USPS's commercial Ground Advantage surcharge now runs roughly a third above the base rate.
- Why operators should care: any 3PL or e-commerce brand still pricing holiday shipping off last year's carrier numbers is already behind, and the highest add-on fees of the entire season land in less than 2 weeks.

## 5. The US-Canada-Mexico trade pact stays unsettled heading into the holidays

- Date: status developing from Jul 1 through early Nov 2026.
- Source: https://www.freightwaves.com/news/usmca-enters-uncertain-future-after-u-s-rejects-automatic-renewal and https://investinglive.com/news/us-trade-rep-greer-eyes-interim-us-deals-with-canada-mexico-by-year-end/
- Summary: The US declined to automatically renew USMCA on Jul 1, 2026, pushing the pact into year-by-year reviews instead of a clean renewal, though current tariff treatment and rules of origin stay in force through 2036 regardless. Talks with Canada broke down around Aug 21, and the US has kept a 50% tariff on close to $20B of Canadian goods in place since Aug 22. US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer is now aiming for narrower interim deals with Canada and Mexico by year end rather than a full renegotiation, with harder issues such as auto rules of origin pushed into 2027.
- Why operators should care: any brand or 3PL sourcing from or shipping through Canada should plan on this tariff staying in place through the holidays, since "interim deals by year end" is a much smaller promise than a resolved trade relationship.
