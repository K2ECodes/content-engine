# Reference Creator Research for Content Systems.ai

Research date: 2026-10-23. Method: Agent Reach (Jina Reader, Exa search, yt-dlp), Instagram public embed pages (`/embed/` and `/embed/captioned/`), and third party analytics mirrors (Postana, igtrackr, Buzzlytics, Viralist). Read only. Nothing was liked, followed, commented on, or logged into.

How to read the numbers: follower counts differ between sources because they were captured on different dates. The most recent and most direct source is listed first. Anything not confirmed from a primary or near primary source is marked **unverified**.

## 7. Synthesis: 14 transferable patterns

Channel key: **KL** = Korede's personal LinkedIn, **CL** = company LinkedIn, **IG** = company Instagram.

1. **One sign off, every time (Jeff). KL.** Jeff ends nearly every post with "Good luck out there / Go forth and operate 👋". It becomes a recognisable signature. Pick one line in Korede's voice tied to operators (for example something about the warehouse floor or shipping day) and use it on every post for 90 days.

2. **Human career takes beat tool posts 2 to 1 (Jeff). KL.** His "give your predecessors a little grace" post got 270 reactions; his sponsored Clay and Sumble posts got 11 to 17. For KL, alternate operator culture opinions (how 3PL teams adopt AI, what ops managers fear) with build posts. Keep tool names in the body, not the hook.

3. **"Agent went wrong, here is the fix" explainers (Jeff). KL, CL.** "Agents left to their own devices without a proper harness and context will hallucinate" plus a concrete numeric failure ($2M quota became $4.8M) and a fix framework did 117 reactions. Our version: a real failure mode in a 3PL quote or carrier email agent, the exact wrong output, and the guardrail (human approval gate, config over memory). This also proves our "human gate" positioning instead of asserting it.

4. **"Steal this 👇" checklists (Jeff). CL.** His 17 metric CRO dashboard list drew the most comments per reaction (56 / 17). Company page posts like "The 12 numbers every 3PL owner should see on Monday" or "Returns dashboard for Shopify brands: steal this" are saveable and easy to produce.

5. **Two tier comment keywords: free item and paid item on the same post (Ajené). IG.** "Comment CUT" for a free skill and "Comment TEMPLATES" for the paid pack. We can run "Comment QUOTE" for a free 3PL quote checklist and point paid intent to the $97 workshop link in bio.

6. **One keyword per offer, repeated (Albert, Ajené). IG.** Albert reuses the same keyword structure dozens of times; Ajené's last six posts use only two keywords. Our last six used five. Pick at most two standing keywords (for example WORKSHOP and AUDIT) and repeat them until the DM automation and audience associate them with us.

7. **Put the keyword in the first line and repeat it if needed (Ajené, Albert). IG.** Their captions often are the CTA ("comment "1%" to find out how to sell AI Agents like these and make $40,000/month doing so", 54.6K likes, 7.3K comments). Our CTAs sit at the end of long captions. Move the comment keyword into the opening line or on screen text, and keep the story below.

8. **Specific money outcome in the hook (Albert, Junaid, Lior). IG, CL.** "$40,000/month", "4 app ideas that could realistically hit $10K+ MRR" (Junaid's best post, 3,029 likes), "Sold my business to Fiverr for $92M with this 47-slide deck". For us: hours saved per week on quotes, dollars recovered from mis-billed shipments, reply time cut. Only use numbers we can back with a client or our own ops.

9. **Carousels for reach, reels for proof (Junaid, Lior). IG.** Junaid's carousels outperform his reels about 4 to 10x; igtrackr shows Lior's carousels at about 27K engagement vs 5.6K for reels. Make list carousels our default IG format ("7 tasks a 3PL can hand to AI this month", "Shopify support emails AI should never send without approval") and use reels for demos.

10. **Curated idea lists as lead magnets (Lior, Junaid). IG.** "Comment "CEO" and I'll send you 200 more startup ideas like these" and "Comment "DOTS" and I'll send you my free guide to the 5 AI agents I'd use to run a one-person business". We can do "the 5 AI employees we'd install first in a 3PL" or "in a Shopify store", carousel shows 3, DM sends all 5.

11. **The work is the content (Ajené). IG, KL.** Every Ajené post is edited by the tool he sells ("This video is only a year old😂😂. Claude edited this video."). Show our systems running: screen recordings of the quote agent, the returns inbox, the approval queue, the "100+ AI employees running our business" from the workshop page. Short, raw, captioned.

12. **Build in public series with a day counter and series hashtag (Junaid). KL.** "Day 01 of Swift UI My Story" (284 reactions) and his #becomeabetterengineer tag. Korede could run "Day N of installing AI in a 3PL" or a named series per client build (anonymised), which also gives a predictable weekly slot.

13. **Plain language for non technical owners (Albert). IG, CL.** Albert's niche is "AI agents to boomer businesses" with concrete jobs: "95% of leads are not converted to a sale... Your AI can find hidden revenue in these leads." Logistics and e-commerce operators are a similar audience. Name the job (quotes, carrier calls, WISMO emails, returns), not the tech (agents, RAG, MCP).

14. **Funnel stack: comment, DM, free community or replay, then call (Albert, Ajené, Jeff). All channels.** Albert: reel, keyword DM, free Skool, coaching. Ajené: reel, keyword DM, VSL page, book a call. Jeff: post, Substack link in profile, podcast guest DMs. Ours should be: post, keyword DM, free resource or workshop replay, $97 workshop, 20 minute call. On LinkedIn, mirror Jeff by pointing to one owned channel (a newsletter) in the profile rather than a hard sell in posts.

**Cadence benchmarks to aim for.** Jeff about 2 to 3 LinkedIn posts per week plus reshares and active commenting. Junaid about 3 IG posts per week. Albert about 2 per week (Postana estimate). Realistic target: KL 3 per week, CL 2 per week (mostly repurposed from KL with company framing), IG 3 per week (2 carousels, 1 reel).

**Immediate fixes for our IG.**
- Reposition the feed around the two verticals (3PL and e-commerce) plus workshops, matching the website.
- Consolidate to two keywords.
- Lead with a carousel series and a weekly demo reel.
- Keep personal tags (#K2E, #flowwithsanj) and first person founder stories for KL and founders' own accounts; use "we" on the company account.
