No Content Systems.ai selling on these.

## Why the format works
- The joke lands in 1 frame: a familiar person or face in an absurd or very ordinary-but-wrong situation. No setup needed.
- Short (13 to 18 s), loops cleanly, 1 punchline, often no captions or 1 meme caption.
- "Recreate this with your own photo" is half the appeal: viewers comment to ask how. For Korede that's the CTA hook: comment AI.
- The human idea carries it; the AI effect is the delivery. Pure AI with no human angle plateaus (vendor blogs agree, see sources).

## House rules for this format
- 10 to 25 s, 9:16, 1 joke, payoff before 12 s, then a beat to land the stare.
- Visual hook in the first second (someone asleep with a hand coming in, a phone at the mouth, a lightning flash).
- Meme captions burned in the lower-middle (y 1180 to 1360 of 1920): white heavy condensed type with black outline, 1 line at a time.
- Korede's voice is his free HeyGen instant clone; other characters (boss, AI assistant, trailer narrator) are kie Gemini TTS voices.
- Music: dark rage-trap bed under skits, horror score under the trailer. Low under dialogue.
- End card: "follow for AI" plus "comment AI". Comment keyword AI in the caption's first 3 lines.
- Keep it AI-themed: tools, prompts, agents, AI glitches. No real people besides Korede, no characters, logos or songs we don't own.
