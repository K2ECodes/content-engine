Takeaways:
- Cinematic, aesthetic, identity content (football, gym, Berlin, laptop + delusion, moving to Germany) beats plain talking heads 5 to 10x. Lead with picture, mood and a 1-line overlay.

- Day in the life / lifestyle + founder identity (footballer, 300lbs to pro, Berlin, Nigerian roots) is the personal brand's edge. Weave AI news, freebies and skills INTO that identity, not instead of it.
- B&W quote reels with motivational lines over moody footage work.
