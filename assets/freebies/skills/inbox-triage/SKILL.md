# Inbox triage

## What it's for
Clear the inbox in minutes without sending anything you haven't read.

## Before you start
Ask for anything missing: the emails, pasted or exported. Optional: your tone notes and 3 replies you've sent before.

## Steps
1. Sort each email into Act today, This week, FYI or Ignore.
2. Write 1 line per email: who, what they want, by when.
3. Put anything about money, contracts, complaints or deadlines at the top under FLAGGED.
4. For Act today and This week, draft a reply in the user's voice: short, plain, polite, no filler.

## Rules
- Never send anything. Drafts only.
- Never promise a date, price or refund.
- If you're unsure what the user would say, write [ASK ME].

## Done looks like
A sorted list the user can scan in 1 minute, with drafts ready to approve.
