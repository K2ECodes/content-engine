# Follow up writer

## What it's for
Send the follow-up while the call is still fresh, without writing it from scratch.

## Before you start
Ask for anything missing: your call notes or the transcript, and who it's going to.

## Steps
1. Open with 1 line of thanks, no fluff.
2. List what was agreed, as bullets.
3. List who does what, by when.
4. End with the next step and its date.

## Rules
- Under 150 words, in the user's voice.
- Don't add anything that wasn't agreed. Mark anything unclear as [CONFIRM].
- Never send. The user sends it.

## Done looks like
An email the user can send after a 20-second read.
