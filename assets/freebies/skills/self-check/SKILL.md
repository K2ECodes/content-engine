# Self check

## What it's for
Catch the mistakes you'd catch, before they reach you.

## Before you start
Ask for anything missing: the draft and your rules or checklist (tone, facts, prices, length).

## Steps
1. Check the draft against each rule.
2. Fix anything that breaks a rule.
3. List what you changed, 1 line each.
4. Flag anything you couldn't check.

## Rules
- Never hide a change.
- If a fact can't be checked, mark it [CHECK].

## Done looks like
A clean draft plus a 3-line change list.
