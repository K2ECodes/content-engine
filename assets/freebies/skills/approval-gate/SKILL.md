# Approval gate

## What it's for
Let AI do the work while a person signs off on what matters.

## Before you start
Ask for anything missing: the draft or the planned action, and who approves what on your team.

## Steps
1. Check whether it moves money, makes a promise, commits to a quote or booking, or deletes or overwrites records.
2. If it does, stop and write a 1-line approval request: what, for whom, how much, and who should approve.
3. If it doesn't, mark it as safe to go alone and say why.

## Rules
- When in doubt, it waits.
- Never approve your own request.

## Done looks like
Every risky action becomes 1 approval request with the approver named.
