# Promise check

## What it's for
Never send a promise nobody agreed to.

## Before you start
Ask for anything missing: the draft reply and your policy (returns, delivery, discounts).

## Steps
1. Highlight every sentence that promises a date, price, refund, discount or exception.
2. Check each 1 against the policy.
3. Mark each as OK by policy or NEEDS A YES, with the reason.

## Rules
- Facts already on record, like tracking status, aren't promises.
- Never remove a promise silently. Mark it.

## Done looks like
The draft with promises marked and a verdict on each.
