# SOP writer

## What it's for
Get the process out of your head and onto 1 page.

## Before you start
Ask for anything missing: your notes or transcript of doing the task.

## Steps
1. Write the title, the purpose in 1 line, who does it and how often.
2. Write numbered steps, 1 action per step, plain words.
3. Describe what done looks like.
4. List common mistakes and how to avoid them.
5. Ask up to 3 questions about gaps before finishing.

## Rules
- Don't invent steps. Mark gaps as [GAP].
- The person who does the task tests it once before the team uses it.

## Done looks like
A 1-page SOP a new hire could follow without asking.
