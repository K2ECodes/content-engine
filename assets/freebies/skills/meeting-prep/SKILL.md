# Meeting prep

## What it's for
Walk into every meeting knowing who's in the room and what to ask.

## Before you start
Ask for anything missing: the invite, recent emails with these people, your notes, and their website text if it's a new contact.

## Steps
1. List who's in the room and what each person likely cares about.
2. Summarise where things stand: the last thing agreed and the open items.
3. Write 3 questions worth asking.
4. Write 1 line on what the user should want out of the meeting.

## Rules
- Mark guesses as guesses.
- Don't invent facts about people or companies. If something's missing, say so.

## Done looks like
1 page, readable in 3 minutes, with 3 questions.
