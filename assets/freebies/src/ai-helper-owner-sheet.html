The 3 questions that decide it

01

Who checks its work? 1 named person, every day. Not "the team".

02

What can it do alone? Write the line down, and where it needs a yes.

What does it report? A short daily note to its owner: what it handled and what it passed on.

An order status helper for an online store. Illustrative, not a client setup. Yours will be shorter or longer, but never blank.

AI helper

Order status replies

Support lead · ops manager

What it does

Answers "where's my order?" emails with live tracking.

Where it works

The support inbox and the store's order list.

Done looks like

Customer has a date and a link, same day.

Off switch

Support lead pauses it in the inbox settings.

Does alone

Replies with tracking status and the delivery date the carrier shows.

Needs a yes

Reships, refunds, any new delivery promise, any upset customer.

Never does

Changes an address, cancels an order, offers a discount.

Daily note

Handled 140 · passed on 11 · corrected 2 · 3 parcels stuck in 1 depot.

4 signs a helper needs its owner back

  • The daily note stopped arriving, or nobody reads it.
  • The same correction shows up 3 days in a row.
  • It's passing on far more, or far fewer, than last month.
  • The owner changed roles, and nobody updated this sheet.
