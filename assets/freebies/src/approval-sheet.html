The Approval Sheet

The 4 actions that always wait

For each 1: what counts, why it waits, and what the AI can still do alone so the yes takes seconds, not minutes.

01

Money out

Approver
What counts

Refunds, credits, supplier payments, price changes and new discount codes.

Why it waits

Money that leaves is hard to get back. At peak, 1 wrong rule repeats across hundreds of orders before anyone looks.

AI can still

Pull the order, check the policy, work out the amount and line it up for 1 click.

02

Customer messages that promise something

Approver
What counts

Delivery dates, discounts, apologies, exceptions to your policy, and any reply to an upset customer.

Why it waits

A promise in writing is a commitment. Routine answers that only repeat facts on record, like tracking status or your returns window, can go alone.

AI can still

Draft the reply in your voice, with the order history attached, and mark the line that makes the promise.

03

Quotes, bookings and contracts

Approver
What counts

Freight bookings, rate quotes, purchase orders, carrier and supplier changes, anything someone signs.

Why it waits

A booking commits a truck and a bill. A quote sets a price you'll be held to.

AI can still

Compare carriers, build the quote, shortlist the best 2 and explain the difference in 1 line.

04

Deleting or overwriting records

Approver
What counts

Deleting or archiving data, overwriting stock counts, merging customer records, closing accounts.

Why it waits

You can't approve what's already gone. A deleted record also leaves a hole in the history you'll need in a dispute.

AI can still

Find duplicates and stale records, suggest the clean-up and mark each 1 for review.

The rule under all 4: if it leaves the building, costs money or can't be undone, a person says yes first. Everything else, the AI can prepare.

How a yes works

DRAFT

The AI prepares the action with everything you need to decide: the order, the policy, the amount.

CHECK

A named person approves, changes or says no. 1 click when it's right, a quick edit when it isn't.

SEND

Only then does it go out. Every step is on record: who approved what, and when.

Signs your yes is too loose

  • Nobody can say who approved the last refund over your limit.
  • The AI logs into tools with a person's login, so its actions look like theirs.
  • Approvals happen in a group chat, with no record of who said yes.
