The 6 lines

Each line shows what to compare, where to find it, and the red flag that says you're paying for something you didn't agree to.

01

Fuel surcharge

$ found
Compare

The fuel rate applied to each shipment vs the carrier's published fuel table for that ship week.

Where to find it

Invoice detail by tracking number, the carrier's weekly fuel table, your agreement's fuel terms.

Red flag

Fuel charged on fees your agreement says are excluded, or a rate that doesn't match the week it shipped.

02

Home delivery and area fees

$ found
Compare

Shipments billed as home deliveries vs business addresses. Remote area fees vs the carrier's current zip code list.

Where to find it

Invoice detail, your order export with ship-to company names, the carrier's area list.

Red flag

A store, office or warehouse address carrying a home delivery fee. A zip that isn't on the list.

03

Address corrections

$ found
Compare

Correction fees vs addresses the carrier actually changed. Was the original address wrong at all?

Where to find it

Correction lines on the invoice, the address on the label, your checkout's address check.

Red flag

Corrections on addresses your checkout already verified, or the same address corrected again and again.

Where to look first: the lines that repeat on every order. A small wrong fee on your bestseller is worth more than a big 1 that happened once.

04

Billed box size

$ found
Compare

The box size the carrier billed vs the box you actually ship. Big, light boxes are billed by size, not weight.

Where to find it

Billed dimensions on the invoice, your packing spec per product, a tape measure on 3 real parcels.

Red flag

A billed size bigger than any box you use. 1 wrong size repeats on every order of that product.

05

Storage and peak fees

$ found
Compare

Your 3PL's peak surcharges and storage lines vs the contract: the dates they start, the rate, what they apply to.

Where to find it

The 3PL contract and its peak addendum, the storage report, your stock on hand by week.

Red flag

Peak fees before the contract date, or storage billed on stock that already shipped.

06

Double and late charges

$ found
Compare

Each tracking number against every invoice this quarter. It should appear once. Then check how old each charge is.

Where to find it

All invoices this quarter in 1 sheet, sorted by tracking number, plus your agreement's billing terms.

Red flag

The same package billed twice, or a charge for shipments from months ago.

How to dispute a line

Send 1 short note per problem, not per parcel: the invoice number, the tracking numbers, the charge, what it should be, and the line in your agreement that says so. Attach your proof.

Check your agreement for the dispute window and send inside it. Carriers and 3PLs usually set a time limit. Log every claim and its answer, so the same error doesn't slip through next week.

Ask for the detail, not the summary

A PDF total can't be checked. Ask your carrier and your 3PL for invoice detail as a spreadsheet, 1 row per shipment or per charge. It's the only way to sort by tracking number, compare box sizes and spot a fee that shouldn't be there.
