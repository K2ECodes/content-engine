3PL Billing Leak Checklist

The 7 checks

Each check shows what to compare, where the data lives, and the red flag that tells you money is slipping.

01

Storage past the free period

$ found
Compare

Pallets and units still on hand after each client's free days vs the storage lines on their invoice.

Where the data lives

Inventory aging report in your WMS, receiving dates, the client's rate card.

Red flag

Aging stock keeps growing, but the storage charge is the same every month.

02

Accessorials and special handling

$ found
Compare

Extra work logged on the floor (oversize, fragile packing, rush orders, weekend shifts, manual labels) vs accessorial lines billed.

Where the data lives

WMS task log, shift notes, client emails and chat requests, the rate card.

Red flag

"Can you rush this?" emails in the inbox, and no rush fee anywhere on the invoice.

03

Kitting and project work

$ found
Compare

Kits and bundles built, assembly, rework and special projects vs project hours and per-kit fees billed.

Where the data lives

Work orders, labor hours by job, the email where the client asked for it.

Red flag

A project tied up a team for 2 days, and the invoice shows a flat fee or nothing.

04

Receiving problems and missing ASNs

$ found
Compare

Inbound that arrived without an advance ship notice (ASN), with wrong counts, or as unlabeled mixed pallets vs receiving exception fees billed.

Where the data lives

Receiving log, ASN records, dock photos, exception notes.

Red flag

Your team spent hours sorting a mixed container, and the line says "standard receiving".

05

Relabels, inserts and prep

$ found
Compare

Relabeling, stickering, inserts, bagging and marketplace prep vs per-unit prep fees billed.

Where the data lives

Special instructions on orders, pack station notes, supply usage.

Red flag

Label and insert stock runs down fast, with no matching charges on any invoice.

06

Disputed invoice credits

$ found
Compare

Credits issued after a dispute vs what the original data shows. Was the dispute valid, and was the corrected amount ever billed?

Where the data lives

Credit memo list, dispute emails, the original invoice and its backup.

Red flag

Credits given to end an argument, without anyone checking the original data.

07

Monthly minimums

$ found
Compare

Each client's monthly total vs the minimum in their contract.

Where the data lives

Signed contracts, billing summary by client.

Red flag

A small client sits under the minimum for months and is billed only for actual usage.

Why it leaks

The work happens on the floor, all month, in lots of small moments. Billing happens at month end, in a spreadsheet, from whatever made it into the system.

Nobody connects the 2 line by line. Peak season makes it worse: more volume, more rush requests, more exceptions, and less time to check.
