Where it breaks between you

The facts rarely go wrong on 1 side. They go wrong in the handover between the brand and the warehouse.

Stock that lags

The warehouse count updates the store once a night. By noon on a sale day, the store is selling stock that's gone.

Vague ship windows

The brand writes "3 to 7 days" because nobody shared the warehouse cut-off and carrier times.

Hidden returns rules

The policy lives in a footer link. The assistant reads unclear fine print as a no.

Details locked in pictures

Size charts and materials sit in images. A shopper that reads text can't see them.

5 questions to ask each other before peak

  • How often does the warehouse stock count reach the store, and what's the lag on a busy day?
  • What's the same-day cut-off this peak, and who updates the delivery promise if it changes?
  • How many days from a return arriving to the refund going out?
  • Who tells the store when an inbound shipment is late, so restock dates move too?
  • If a fact is wrong on the store, who fixes it, and how fast?
