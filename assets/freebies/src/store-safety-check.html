The Store Safety Check

3 checks, about 10 minutes

Tick each box. If you can't tick 1, send this page to your host or developer and ask them to.

01

Ask your host or developer

Done on
Ask

"Was PHP on our site updated after October 21? Which version are we on now?" Get the answer in writing.

Hosted store?

If your store runs on a hosted platform, check its status page or ask support whether its servers were patched.

Note

Write down the date they confirm. You'll want it if anything shows up later.

02

Update everything that runs your store

Done on
What

Your store platform, every plugin and theme, and any old test or staging site still online.

Why

Old copies of a site are the easiest way in. If nobody uses it, take it offline.

Before you update

Make sure there's a recent backup you can restore.

03

Look for signs since October 17

Done on
Accounts

Admin users nobody on your team created. Remove them and change passwords.

Money

Payout or payment details nobody on your team changed, and orders or refunds you don't recognise.

Files

Ask your developer for a list of files changed since October 17 that nobody can explain.

Found something? Don't delete it yourself. Tell your host or developer straight away, and keep notes of what you saw and when.
