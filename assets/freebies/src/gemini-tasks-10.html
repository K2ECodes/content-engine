10 tasks you can hand Gemini Tasks today

Google made Tasks, its AI agent, free on Nov 17, 2026. You give it a job, it works in the background and pings you when it's done. Swap the [brackets] for your details and paste.

THE LINK

gemini.google.com

Or the Gemini app on Android, iPhone or Mac. Free in the US, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Australia and India.

STEP 1

Flip the switch from Chat to Task (above the prompt box on mobile, below it on the web).

STEP 2

Describe the goal, add a schedule if it repeats, submit. You'll get a notification when it's done.

01

Flight price watch

Daily
Check flights from [city] to [city] for [dates] every morning. Tell me as soon as a fare drops under $[price], with the airline, stops and a link.
02

Morning brief

Daily
Every weekday at 7am, read my email and calendar and give me a 5-line brief: today's meetings, deadlines, and the emails that need a reply today.
03

The surprise bill

My [provider] bill was much higher this month. Go through my past bills, tell me why it went up, and give me 2 ways to bring it back down.
04

Compare and buy

US
Find the best-rated [item] under $[price], look for a discount code, and get it ready to check out with Google Pay. Don't pay until I approve.
05

Get 3 quotes by phone

US
Call 3 [plumbers / cleaners / mechanics] near [zip code] and get quotes for [the job] this week. Show me the plan before you call, then the cheapest open slot.
06

Weekend trip plan

Plan 3 days in [city] for [dates]: flights from [city], a hotel under $[price] a night near [area], and a day-by-day plan with places to eat.
07

Hotel price watch

Daily
Once a day, check hotels in [city] for [dates]. Tell me if a 4-star with 8+ reviews drops under $[price] a night.
08

Inbox clean-up

Find the newsletters and promos I haven't opened in the last 60 days. List them with how often they email me, so I can pick what to unsubscribe from.
09

Weekly grocery order

US
Build my grocery order on Instacart from this list: [list]. Pick the best value for each item and show me the cart before checkout.
10

Monday research digest

Weekly
Every Monday, find last week's news about [topic or competitor] and send me 5 bullets, each with 1 line on why it matters and a link.

The 1 thing to watch

Tasks can read your email, calendar and saved passwords to get things done. It asks before it buys, sends, deletes or signs in. Set a spending cap, and don't tap "Always allow" on purchases or messages.

Free plan limits

18+, personal Google account. Scheduled tasks run at most once a day, up to 10 schedules. Calling, buying and texting Gemini are US only for now. Not in the EU, UK or Switzerland yet.
