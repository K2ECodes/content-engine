Agent-Ready Store Checklist

Tick what's true today

Not what's planned. Check each line on your live store, the way a stranger would see it.

01

Clear product facts

An assistant compares products line by line. Facts it can't find count as "unknown".

  • Every product title plainly names the item: brand, product and the key option, not just a clever name.
  • Size, material, dimensions, weight and what it works with are written as text, not only shown in photos.
  • Each option (size, color) has its own correct details, photo and stock.
  • Product codes and barcode numbers are filled in and match your stock system.
  • 1 line says who it's for, and what it isn't for.
02

Real stock

Back-in-stock alerts and "buy it for me" requests only work if your numbers are right.

  • Stock on your site matches what's on the shelf, updated at least daily.
  • Sold-out items show as sold out, not as available with a quiet delay.
  • Restock dates only appear when you're sure of them.
  • Counts from your warehouse or 3PL reach your store automatically, not by hand.
03

Honest delivery times

"Fast shipping" means nothing to a machine. A date range does.

  • Delivery is shown as a date range ("arrives Tue to Thu"), not a promise like "fast shipping".
  • The cut-off time for same-day dispatch is stated.
  • Holiday order-by dates are posted before peak season starts.
  • Shipping costs show before checkout, by region.

04

Returns in plain words

If the policy needs a lawyer to read it, an assistant treats it as a risk.

  • The return window is stated in days, in 1 sentence.
  • It says who pays for return shipping.
  • It lists what can't be returned.
  • It says how long a refund takes once the item arrives back.
  • The same policy text appears on your site, marketplaces and product pages.
05

Answers to common questions

Assistants answer shoppers' questions before they ever reach you. Give them your answer to quote.

  • Your FAQ answers the 10 questions customers actually ask. Pull them from your support inbox.
  • Each answer starts with the answer, then the detail.
  • Sizing or fit help, care instructions and warranty terms are on the product page.
  • Answers are written as text on the page, not locked in a PDF or an image.
06

Same price everywhere

Universal Cart and similar tools compare prices across stores. A mismatch looks like a mistake or a trick.

  • Price and sale price match across your site, marketplaces and Google listings.
  • Sale prices end when the sale ends, on every channel.
  • Promo codes have clear terms written next to them.
  • Taxes, duties and fees are clear before checkout.
07

Reviews

Reviews are the proof an assistant can point to when it recommends you.

  • Reviews show on product pages as text, with a star rating and a count.
  • Every buyer gets a review request after delivery, so reviews stay recent.
  • Negative reviews get a calm, public reply that says what you fixed.
  • Nothing is filtered or faked. Shoppers and assistants both notice.

Agent-Ready Store Checklist

Short on time? Start here

3 fixes that take about an hour and cover the facts assistants check most.

FIX 1

Check your 5 bestsellers. Does the stock on your site match the shelf? Fix the ones that don't, then find out why.

FIX 2

Replace "fast shipping" with a real date range and a same-day cut-off time on every product page.

FIX 3

Rewrite your returns policy in 4 sentences: the window, who pays, what's excluded, how long refunds take.
